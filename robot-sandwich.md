# PB&J Sandwich

## Ingredients
- 2 slices of bread
- Peanut butter
- Jelly

## Instructions
1. Pick up first slice of bread
2. Apply adn spread the peanut butter to the first slice
3. Pick up second slice of bread
4. Apply adn spread the jelly to the second slice
5. Put both slices together with the applied sides touching

## Done When
- Both of the slices of bread have either peanut butter or jelly on them and then put both slices together with the peanut butter and jelly sides
